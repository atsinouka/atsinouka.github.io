I am **Angeliki Tsinouka**, a PhD candidate at the
[National Technical University of Athens (NTUA)](https://www.ntua.gr/en/),
affiliated with the
[HERON – Hellenic Robotics Center of Excellence](https://heron-robotics-coe.eu/),
under the supervision of Prof.
[Petros Maragos](https://robotics.ntua.gr/members/maragos/).

My research interests lie at the intersection of **Computer Vision,
Machine Learning, and Robotics**, with a particular focus on robotic
perception, 3D scene understanding, object pose estimation, and
robotic manipulation.

I received my Diploma in Electrical and Computer Engineering from
NTUA in 2025. My diploma thesis,
*[Occlusion-Robust Audiovisual Face Reconstruction with Temporal Modeling](https://dspace.lib.ntua.gr/xmlui/handle/123456789/64359?show=full)*,
was conducted under the supervision of Prof. Petros Maragos.
