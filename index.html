Angeliki Tsinouka

PhD Student @ NTUA, HERON

I am a first-year PhD student at the National Technical University of Athens (NTUA), affiliated with Hellenic Robotics Center of Excellence (HERON), under the supervision of Prof. Petros Maragos. Before starting my PhD, I received my Diploma M.Eng Degree from the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering at NTUA in 2025. My diploma thesis focused on audiovisual face reconstruction, under the supervision of Prof. Petros Maragos.

My research interests include Computer Vision, Machine Learning, and Robotics, particularly robotic perception, 3D scene understanding, object pose estimation, and robotic manipulation.
